Ingredients

  • 500g pork belly
  • 1.5whole red onion
  • 1stalk lemongrass
  • 2tbsp fish sauce
  • 2tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1tbsp sesame oil
  • 0.33tsp salt
  • 0.33tsp sugar
  • 0.33tsp MSG
  • 1pcs yellow onion
  • sesame seeds
  • 1.5tsp honey

Instructions

  1. Finely chop half of the red onion and the lemongrass.
  2. Add the chopped red onion and lemongrass to the pork belly.
  3. Add the fish sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, sugar, and MSG. Mix and knead thoroughly until the pork is evenly coated.
  4. Cover and marinate for 30 minutes.
  5. Cut the remaining red onion and the yellow onion into thin, flat slices.
  6. Thread the pork and onion alternately onto skewers. Follow this order: onion, lean piece of pork, onion, fatty piece of pork.
  7. Preheat the oven to 230°C.
  8. Meanwhile, lightly toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan. Mix the honey with a little water to make a thin glaze.
  9. Roast the skewers until they look cooked and lightly browned but are not burnt. Brush them with the honey-water glaze and sprinkle with the toasted sesame seeds.
  10. Turn the skewers over and repeat the glazing and sesame step on the other side.
  11. Return the skewers to the oven for another 2–3 minutes, until the pork is fully cooked and nicely caramelized.
