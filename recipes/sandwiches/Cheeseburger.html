Ingredients

  • 2pcs burger buns
  • 300g ground beef
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • oil
  • 2slices Cheddar cheese (optional)
  • iceberg lettuce
  • 1pcs tomato
  • 1pcs onion
  • 1 pickled cucumber
  • burger sauce
  • 2tbsp butter

Instructions

  1. Form two burger patties using 150 g of ground beef for each patty.
  2. Season both sides of the patties generously with salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat some oil in a frying pan.
  4. Place the patties in the hot pan and sear them on both sides until cooked through.
  5. Meanwhile, cut the onion into rings, slice the tomato and pickled cucumber, and prepare a few iceberg lettuce leaves.
  6. Add 1 tbsp of butter to the pan and fry half the onion rings until golden brown. Stir the onions occasionally and season them with salt.
  7. Towards the end, add the pickled cucumber slices to the onions.
  8. When the patties are almost cooked through, place one slice of Cheddar on each patty and let it melt.
  9. Once the pickles have become soft and tender, place them on top of the patties.
  10. Remove the patties together with their toppings from the pan.
  11. Add 1 tbsp of butter to the pan and toast the bun halves cut-side down.
  12. Finally, wipe the pan clean with the burger buns.
  13. Spread burger sauce on the cut sides of the buns. Place two tomato slices and some iceberg lettuce on the bottom halves. Add the remaining toppings and assemble the burgers.
  14. Tip: For a homemade burger sauce, mix ketchup, mayonnaise, mustard, pickle brine, chopped pickles, salt, pepper and similar ingredients to taste.
  15. Note: without cheese, this recipe is a hamburger recipe. With the cheese, this becomes a cheeseburger recipe.
