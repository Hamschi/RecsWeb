Ingredients

  • 1bag nachos
  • 200g cheese (e.g. Cheddar, Pepper Jack, Mozzarella,...)
  • 500g Taco Beef
  • 450ml Cheese Sauce
  • 2pcs tomato
  • 2pcs white onion
  • black beans
  • pickled jalapeno
  • guacamole
  • crème fraîche (alternatively sour cream)

Instructions

  1. If haven't done yet: prepare the cheese sauce and the taco beef
  2. Preheat the oven at 150°C.
  3. Dice the onion, cut the tomato into little cubes, slice the pickled jalapeno and shred the cheese.
  4. Put a layer of nachos in a baking tray followed by a generous spinkle of cheese and taco beef, a layer of cheese sauce, black beans to taste, chopped onions and tomatoes and jalapenos.
  5. Repeat the last step 2-3 times until the baking tray is full.
  6. Bake the nachos for 20 minutes.
  7. Meanwhile (if haven't done yet): Prepare the guacamole
  8. After the 20 minutes: serve with crème fraîche and guacamole.
