Ingredients

  • 12gcorn starch
  • 88gflour
  • 0.125tspbaking powder
  • 75mldashi stock
  • 1tspmilk
  • 1tspsoy sauce
  • 1tspmirin
  • 1pcsegg (large)
  • 150gcabbage
  • 2pcsegg
  • 2tbspcooking oil
  • 100gpork belly (thinly sliced)
  • Okonomiyaki Sauce (alternatively Tonkatsu sauce)
  • Japanese mayonnaise
  • bonito flakes (optional)
  • aonori (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix the corn starch, flour and baking powder into a large bowl
  2. Add the dashi stock, milk, soy sauce, mirin and the egg(s) and mix until the ingredients are well incorporated. Be careful not to overmix it.
  3. Cover the bowl and rest it in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  4. Cut the green cabbage into into small rough pieces.
  5. Once the batter has been resting for 30 minutes, pour half of it into a new bowl.
  6. Place the original bowl back in the fridge for later.
  7. Start preheating your pan on a medium/medium-high setting.
  8. While you wait, add half of the cabbage to the bowl of batter. If you want to add other ingredients such as tempura flakes, red pickled ginger, cheese, kimchi, mochi etc add them here.
  9. Mix until everything is evenly distributed.
  10. Add 1 egg and mix again until it's incorporated. Be careful not to over mix.
  11. Add the oil to the pan and swirl around until evenly coated. Wipe out the excess with kitchen paper and pour the okonomiyaki mixture into the center of the pan.
  12. Use a spatula to neaten up the edges.
  13. Lay the slices of pork belly over the top of the wet mixture and fry the okonomiyaki until the bottom starts to become brown and crispy.
  14. Carefully flip it over and place a lid on the pan.
  15. Reduce the heat to medium-low and continue to fry with the lid for about 5 minutes or until cooked all the way through.
  16. Pierce the middle with a toothpick to make sure it's cooked. If the toothpick doesn't come out clean, continue to cook on a low heat and check it every few minutes until it's done.
  17. Once it's cooked through, flip it over again and put it on a plate
  18. Put Okonomiyaki sauce evenly on the pancake. Use a pastry brush or spoon to spread it evenly. The whole surface should be covered with sauce.
  19. Drizzle with mayonnaise and sprinkle generously with bonito flakes and aonori.
  20. Repeat the steps 8-19 for the other bowl of batter.
