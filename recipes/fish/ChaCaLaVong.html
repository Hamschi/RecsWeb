Ingredients

  • 450g fish
  • 25g galangal
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 chili
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 10g fresh turmeric
  • 0.5tbsp chicken bouillon powder
  • 1pinch MSG
  • 1tbsp oil
  • 1tbsp Mắm Tôm (paste)
  • 1tsp sugar
  • 25g cơm mẻ (alternatively yogurt)
  • black pepper
  • 100g spring onions
  • 100g dill

Instructions

  1. Cut the fish into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Cut the galangal into slices about 1 mm thick and roughly chop them in a food processor.
  3. Add the garlic, shallot and chili and roughly process everything together.
  4. Cut the fresh turmeric into slices about 1 mm thick and roughly crush it in a mortar.
  5. Add the cơm mẻ (or yogurt) and a little water to the turmeric and mix well.
  6. Strain the mixture through a sieve. For a more intense color, add some turmeric powder if desired.
  7. Add the turmeric liquid, the chopped galangal-garlic-shallot-chili mixture, Mắm Tôm, chicken bouillon powder, MSG, oil and black pepper to the fish and mix well. Marinate the fish for at least 30 minutes.
  8. Quarter the spring onions and dill. Cut the white parts of the spring onions into thin strips.
  9. Cover the bottom of the air fryer basket densely with dill and spring onions.
  10. Arrange the fish on top and air fry at 180°C for 8 minutes.
  11. Turn the fish over and air fry for another 5 minutes at 180°C.
  12. Heat some oil in a pan and fry all of the dill and spring onions.
  13. Add the fish to the vegetables and briefly sear it on both sides.
  14. Serve Chả Cá Lã Vọng with bún and Mắm Tôm.
