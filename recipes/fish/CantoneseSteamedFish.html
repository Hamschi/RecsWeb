Ingredients

  • 400g white fish fillets
  • salt
  • Shaoxing wine
  • 35g ginger
  • 5pcs spring onions
  • 2tbsp soy sauce
  • 1.5tsp sugar
  • 1tsp sesame oil
  • 3tbsp warm water
  • 3tbsp vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Optional: Cut the fish into bite-sized pieces
  2. Sprinkle the top of the fish with salt and drizzle with Shaoxing wine.
  3. Peel the ginger and cut it into thin slices.
  4. Roughly cover a deep plate with some of the ginger slices.
  5. Cut the remaining ginger into very thin strips.
  6. Quarter the spring onions lengthwise.
  7. Roughly cover the plate with some of the spring onions.
  8. Cut the remaining spring onions into very thin strips.
  9. Place the fish on the plate.
  10. Steam the fish over medium heat for 5–10 minutes, until cooked through.
  11. Mix the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and warm water in a bowl to make the sauce.
  12. Once the fish is cooked, pour away the liquid that has collected on the plate.
  13. Place the spring onion and ginger strips on top of the fish and pour the sauce over it.
  14. Heat the oil over high heat until very hot, then pour it over the fish.
