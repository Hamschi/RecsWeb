Ingredients

  • 15pcs gelatin leaves
  • 300ml whole milk
  • 80g sugar
  • 1pinch salt
  • 400ml heavy cream
  • 0.5tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.225tsp pandan extract
  • 100g rock sugar
  • 1l water
  • 5pcs pandan leaves
  • 1pinch salt
  • 40g sliced almonds
  • 1can canned longan in syrup
  • 1tbsp chia seeds

Instructions

  1. Soak the gelatin leaves in cold water for 10 minutes.
  2. Place the milk, sugar and salt in a saucepan and heat over medium heat, stirring until the sugar and salt have dissolved.
  3. Squeeze the excess water from the gelatin leaves and place them in a saucepan.
  4. Heat the gelatin over low heat until melted. Turn off the heat as soon as the gelatin starts to melt.
  5. Add the melted gelatin to the milk and stir thoroughly.
  6. Add the heavy cream and heat over medium heat, stirring until the mixture is completely smooth and homogeneous.
  7. Optionally, strain the mixture through a sieve.
  8. Divide the milk mixture evenly between two separate containers.
  9. Stir the vanilla extract into one half and the pandan extract into the other.
  10. Allow the milk mixtures to cool for 1 hour.
  11. Cover the containers and refrigerate overnight until the mixtures are fully set.
  12. Place the rock sugar, water, pandan leaves and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring until the sugar has dissolved.
  13. Allow the syrup to cool completely, then refrigerate it.
  14. Toast the sliced almonds in a pan over medium heat until lightly browned.
  15. Once the jelly has set, take 150 ml of syrup from the longan can and stir the chia seeds into it. Let them soak until hydrated.
  16. Cut the set jelly into bite-sized pieces.
  17. Place the jelly pieces, chia seeds, longan, pandan rock sugar syrup and toasted almonds in a bowl and serve.
