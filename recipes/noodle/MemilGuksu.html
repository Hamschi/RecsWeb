Ingredients

  • 200g soba noodles
  • 0.5pcs cucumber
  • 2pcs spring onions
  • 1sheet roasted seaweed (gim/nori)
  • 140g daikon radish
  • 0.5pcs onion
  • 1pcs carrot
  • 1small piece kombu
  • 8tbsp soy sauce
  • 6tbsp sugar
  • 1l water
  • 2tbsp katsuobushi (bonito flakes)
  • wasabi (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cut the onion into rough pieces. Cut the daikon radish and carrot into medium-sized slices. Halve the spring onions and set them aside.
  2. Place the onion, daikon radish and carrot in a large pot. Gently wipe the kombu with a dry cloth and add it to the pot. Add the soy sauce, sugar and the water.
  3. Bring everything to a boil over medium-high heat.
  4. Reduce the heat to medium and simmer the broth for about 15 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat, add the katsuobushi and let it steep for about 5 minutes.
  6. Pour the broth through a sieve and discard the vegetables, kombu and bonito flakes. Allow the broth to cool completely, then refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.
  7. Cut the cucumber into thin strips. Finely slice the spring onions and cut the roasted seaweed into thin strips.
  8. Cook the soba noodles according to the package instructions
  9. Drain the noodles and rinse them several times under cold running water. Gently rub them with your hands while rinsing to remove excess starch.
  10. Divide the well-chilled broth between bowls. Add the noodles and garnish with cucumber, spring onions and seaweed.
  11. Optional: Add some wasabi to taste and enjoy the noodles in the cold broth
