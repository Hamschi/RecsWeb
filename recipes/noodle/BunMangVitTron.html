Ingredients

  • 1.5l water
  • 500g duck legs
  • 1.5tsp salt
  • 1.5pcs onions
  • 20g ginger
  • 1tsp chicken bouillon powder
  • fish sauce
  • Mọc meatballs
  • spring onions
  • cilantro
  • Mùi tàu (optional)
  • 200g bamboo shoots
  • 2tbsp fish sauce
  • black pepper
  • bún rice noodles
  • 3cloves garlic
  • 1pcs shallot
  • 1pcs chili
  • 1tbsp sugar
  • 4tbsp soy sauce
  • lime juice
  • 1tsp chicken bouillon powder
  • 50ml water (alternatively duck broth)
  • black pepper
  • oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Bring the water in a pot to a boil.
  2. Place the duck legs in the pot together with salt, onions and ginger
  3. Simmer the duck for about 1 hour. Depending on the size of the duck legs, cooking may take slightly longer. The duck is ready when the meat is tender.
  4. Boil the bamboo shoots 2–3 times, each time for about 10 minutes, using fresh water for each cooking cycle.
  5. Sauté the bamboo shoots in a pan with fish sauce and black pepper.
  6. Once the duck has been cooking for about 1 hour, add the bamboo shoots and Mọc meatballs to the pot.
  7. Season the broth to taste with fish sauce and chicken bouillon powder.
  8. While the broth continues to simmer, prepare the bún rice noodles according to the package instructions.
  9. Finely slice the mùi tàu and the green parts of the spring onions into rings. Cut the white parts of the spring onions lengthwise into thin strips. Roughly cut or chop the cilantro.
  10. Remove the cooked duck from the pot and separate the meat from the bones. Cut or pull the meat into bite-sized pieces.
  11. For the sauce, finely chop the garlic, shallot, and fresh chili.
  12. Heat a little oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and shallot until lightly browned.
  13. Add the sugar, soy sauce, lime juice, Hạt nêm, water, and black pepper.
  14. Simmer the sauce for about 1 minute, then remove from the heat.
  15. Divide the bún rice noodles between serving bowls.
  16. Top with spring onions, mùi tàu and cilantro if desired.
  17. Add the duck meat, Mọc meatballs and the bamboo shoots.
  18. Pour the hot broth with the bamboo shoots over the noodles and serve.
  19. Drizzle the prepared sauce over the noodles according to taste and mix everything thoroughly.
  20. Serve each portion with a separate bowl of the hot duck broth alongside.
