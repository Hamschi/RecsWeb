Ingredients

  • 300gpasta (e.g. Fettucine, Tagliatelle)
  • 1pcsshallot
  • 1glovegarlic
  • 1knobbutter
  • 100mlwhite wine
  • 400mlcream
  • lemon zest
  • 1sprinklelemon juice
  • 150gsalmon
  • 1handfuldill
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Prepare the noodles in a pot and cook them al dente. Keep the noodle water.
  2. Finely dice the shallot, garlic and dill. Cut the salmon into small stripes or cubes.
  3. Fry the shallot and the garlic in the butter until fragrant.
  4. Deglaze the shallot and the garlic using white wine.
  5. Whenever the wine is almost evaporated completely, add the cream and bring it to a boil.
  6. Add pepper and salt to taste. Keep in mind to use less salt if you are using smoked salmon.
  7. Add lemon zest and lemon juice.
  8. As soon as the sauce thickens, add dill and salmon to the sauce and mix well.
  9. Add the noodles to the sauce. If the sauce got too thick, add noodle water to the sauce. Mix everything well.
  10. Spinkle with more dill for garnish
