Ingredients

  • 3pcs avocado
  • 1pcs tomato
  • 1clove garlic
  • 1pcs red onion
  • 1pcs lime
  • 1tsp cumin
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 0.5pcs jalapeno (optional)
  • 2tbsp cilantro

Instructions

  1. Cut the tomato, remove the flesh and cut into cubes.
  2. Mince or crush the garlic and dice the onion. Finely chop the cilantro and the jalapeno.
  3. Slice the avocados in half, remove the pit, and scoop into a mixing bowl.
  4. Mash the avocado with a fork and make it as chunky or smooth as you’d like.
  5. Add the remaining ingredients and stir together. Give it a taste test and add a pinch more salt or lime juice if needed.
