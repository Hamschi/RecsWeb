Ingredients

  • 1pcs egg yolk
  • 1tbsp cane sugar
  • 1pinch salt
  • 0.33tsp lime juice
  • 50ml vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Add cane sugar, salt and lime juice to the egg yolk to a small bowl and whisk until well combined.
  2. Pour water into a pot until it reaches halfway up the bowl and bring it to a boil. After that turn off the stove.
  3. Put the bowl with the egg yolk into the pot and whisk for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the oil a little at a time and stir thoroughly until the oil is used up.
